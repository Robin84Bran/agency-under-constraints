2026-11-12 HKT. Post hoc questions for possible separately registered work; none changes this completed study or authorizes more collection.

- Why did mathematically redundant worked examples lower Qwen 7B's exact optimal-choice rate by 13 points under independent damage? Is the change driven by added length, attention to failure-free receipts, or a different interpretation of the objective? The present pattern is only consistent with these mechanisms; it does not distinguish them.

- Claude's 118 valid choices are all optimal, and its two technical misses occur on the same block in opposite arms. Would a separately registered study with a different output-accounting budget distinguish a planning ceiling from a provider-output bottleneck? The present failures reveal no wrong chosen fleet.
- How much of the local gap to exact code policies is arithmetic, feasibility recognition, or selecting the probability objective rather than mean work or outage? The existing baseline comparisons describe the gap but do not identify its cause.
- Would the direction survive genuinely sequential execution with queues, returns, repair or correlated partial damage? The one-wave simulator cannot answer that question.
- Is representation sensitivity stable across repeated generations? These frozen-grid intervals condition on one saved reply per prompt; they do not estimate full generation variance.
