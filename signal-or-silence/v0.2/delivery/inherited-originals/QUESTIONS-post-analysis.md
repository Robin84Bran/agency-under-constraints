# Signal or Silence · post-analysis questions

Append-only. These questions were selected **post hoc** on 11 November 2026 after
the first frozen analysis. They are hypotheses for a separately agreed, frozen
study, not conclusions or authorization for new collection. QUESTIONS.md remains
unchanged.

## 2026-11-11 · Frozen v0.1 results, Codex

- Why did Claude abstain on 97.50% of truncated-history views but 0% of
  withheld-authorization views under the plain instruction? Does “unavailable”
  authorization get interpreted as absent authorization, while explicit omitted
  events trigger uncertainty? A future wording/marker manipulation could
  distinguish these possibilities without claiming that this study isolated them.
- Why did the explicit instruction raise Claude W abstention to 88.33% while
  leaving T near its existing ceiling, yet reduce Llama T abstention from 37.50%
  to 12.08% and raise decisive F/R false alarms to 90.00%/95.83%? Which instruction
  clauses or task interpretations matter? Those changes are consistent with
  instruction/presentation sensitivity, not proof of a shared internal mechanism.
- How much of a judge's usable reliability comes from semantic inference versus
  compliance with a machine-readable output contract? Claude had 275 scored
  technical failures across unique calls, including explanatory prose plus JSON
  and the retained D1 overrun flag. A new prospective comparison could enforce
  equivalent output constraints across transports. This does not license a
  permissive rescore or rescue of the present frozen outcomes.
- Which repeated evidence patterns drive trajectory versus equal-evidence gaps?
  For example, explicit Qwen R recall is 60.83% by trajectory and 83.33% by
  decisive-payload weighting; its R abstention is 44.17% versus 23.01%. A larger
  unseen-template benchmark could test whether the gap persists without treating
  repeated seeds or inputs as independent tasks.
- Would judges seek the particular missing receipt that resolves ambiguity in a
  sequential evidence-request task, rather than make a definite accusation or
  repeatedly ask for irrelevant evidence? Fixed-view abstention cannot establish
  useful evidence-seeking behavior.
- Does categorical logical possibility remain useful when histories have highly
  unequal prior probabilities or receipts are untrusted? The current witness test
  establishes possibility under a trusted simulator, not equal likelihood,
  probability calibration or real-world receipt authenticity.
