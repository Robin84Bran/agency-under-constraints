# Signal or Silence · open questions

Append-only dated entries. Questions are hypotheses, not results.

## 2026-11-11 · Initial survey, Codex

- Does UNKNOWN track logical insufficiency, report–receipt conflict, or discomfort with accusing an agent? These can produce the same aggregate abstention rate.
- Why did the GPT breach monitor abstain on five complete HA traces while the differently instructed referee resolved compliance on all 360? Instructions, context and sampling changed; an archived comparison cannot identify the cause.
- Is the large reduction in report-only abstention under the referee prompt useful resolution or confident trust in a false report? Evaluate correctness and epistemic sufficiency separately.
- Can a judge distinguish missing decisive receipts from a missing fallible report? A blanket missingness heuristic could look calibrated in a two-arm experiment.
- What counts as “possible” when the approval condition is hidden? An epistemic witness may vary that condition; a causal intervention holding the original simulator fixture fixed asks a different question.
- How many genuinely different evidence patterns survive truncation and masking? A large trajectory count can conceal a very small language/logic benchmark.
- Can a monitor say UNKNOWN for the right reason while still being operationally useless because it abstains on too many decisive breaches? Keep abstention-as-miss detection co-primary.
- Would receipts that reveal only a probability of approval require graded confidence rather than categorical UNKNOWN? This protocol tests logical ambiguity only.
- Does deliberately deceptive reporting change abstention calibration? The saved actors' intent was not measured, so their false claims cannot establish deception.
- When the sibling's Qwen/Llama judgments become available, do they add a new pattern or simply reproduce source/template effects? Import them descriptively without making their timing a dependency.
- A future sequential version could measure how many additional receipts a judge requests before deciding. Would it seek decisive evidence, or merely accumulate corroboration? Outside this version's fixed-view design and budget.

## 2026-11-11 · Agreement round r2, Codex

- Does an explicit opposite-history sufficiency rule improve useful abstention, or mainly increase null responses while decisive-evidence detection still fails? Compare identical payloads under plain and explicit instructions, with separate joint pass decisions.
- Does a tighter enforced frontier output budget change technical validity or the plain/explicit contrast? Record limit stops and counted reasoning; feasibility and medium-effort compatibility must be resolved offline before freeze, without probing experimental outcomes or changing the cap after results.
- How well do short, heavily cached sibling monitor timings predict fresh, longer explicit-instruction workloads? The saved metadata provides a runtime proxy; later measure actual cache/input/output usage and active time without extending the fixed resource cap.
