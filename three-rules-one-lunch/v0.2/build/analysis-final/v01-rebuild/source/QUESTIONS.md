# Three Rules, One Lunch · Open questions

Append-only dated entries. Questions and possible extensions are not findings, approved extra scope or reasons to change a frozen study.

## 2026-11-11 (HKT) · Survey and v0.1 draft, Codex

- **Should this become a Minimum Intelligence module?** The programme source recommends merging the direction. The draft isolates receipt visibility; review should decide whether that yields a useful standalone question or a component for the existing programme. No additional study is implicitly authorized.
- **How much of the toy's central advantage comes from mobility?** Its central learner jumps anywhere and walkers move one cell. Giving all model arms equal access removes that particular asymmetry but changes the teaching environment; it cannot decompose the old saved result causally.
- **Does faithful sharing improve useful search or just synchronize exploitation?** Repeated food queries are productive in a non-depleting world. Unique coverage and within-round duplicates need reward context; neither automatically measures good coordination.
- **What do learned notes add beyond raw receipts?** The draft makes sharing truthful and lossless. Compression, selective notes, stale summaries and planted misinformation belong in separately registered versions, with provenance and information budgets measured.
- **Is one central call versus three worker calls the right practical comparison?** Equal observations do not equalize model tokens, latency or internal planning. This draft reports those resources; a later cost-matched design would need a separate estimand and pass rule.
- **What survives a longer horizon or unannounced change?** Four rounds and an announced relocation make this a small bounded test. Decaying traces, longer memory, movement restrictions, delayed rewards and depletion could change rankings. They are outside v0.1.
- **Can sharing look beneficial because private workers fail to produce actions?** The all-slot scores, separate abstention/technical counts, validity gate and complete-pair reward sensitivity guard against this. None turns an unknown action into a known environmental failure.
- **Does the 500/1000 harvest threshold capture a useful distinction?** It supplies an abstention-as-miss co-primary but low rewards can be valuable exploration. Publish raw reward alongside it; do not tune the threshold after seeing replies.
- **Will the twenty frontier worlds add information?** Sixty episodes meet the Receipt Gap review's requested scale and permit twenty-block descriptive intervals. They still do not establish a family-wide architecture ranking, and cannot replace the 100-seed local primary.
- **Can the installed ClaudeCLI enforce the proposed accounted-output ceiling without invisible repair/retry calls?** Offline help confirms schema/JSON flags and binary inspection finds structured_output, but enforcement and runtime payload success remain UNKNOWN. Resolve from installed implementation/documentation before frontier collector work; no current model probe is authorized.
- **How far does SoS D1's hidden-input allowance transfer?** The draft measures its own visible/system/schema bytes, but 826 hidden tokens is a historical maximum. Structured-output scaffolding and CLI version changes may require larger holds. Preserve the USD25 envelope and sixty-episode information floor rather than silently relaxing either.
- **How much uncertainty is hidden by one response per prompt?** Seed-block intervals cover synthetic-world variation conditional on saved model responses. They do not measure repeat-generation variance; no unplanned replay or replacement is included here.
